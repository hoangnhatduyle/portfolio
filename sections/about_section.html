International Student

Growing up in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, I developed a strong sense of resilience and adaptability that has shaped every chapter of my career. Leaving home to study in the United States was not just an academic decision — it was a commitment to growth. I carry Vietnamese values of perseverance and community into every team I join, and I take pride in representing my culture while contributing to a global industry.

Growth Mindset

I believe that the best engineers are the ones who never stop being students. Whether it is exploring a new framework after hours, earning a certification, or simply asking better questions in code review — I invest deliberately in my own development. I set specific, measurable goals for myself each quarter and track them with the same rigor I bring to a sprint backlog. Continuous improvement is not a platitude for me; it is a practice.

Engineer at Heart

My journey in software began long before my first professional role. At Nysus Solutions over three years, I maintained and extended manufacturing execution systems for more than 30 customers, working across JavaScript, PHP, HTML, SQL, and PLC integrations. What drives me is the full picture — front to back, UI to database — which is why I gravitate toward full-stack work. Programming is not just my profession; it has been my most consistent tool for solving real problems for real people.

Future Vision

I am working toward a future where I can lead engineering teams building systems that meaningfully improve manufacturing and industrial operations. My goal is not simply to advance in title — it is to become the kind of engineer who mentors others, architects durable solutions, and bridges the gap between shop floor reality and cutting-edge software. I want to leave things measurably better than I found them.
